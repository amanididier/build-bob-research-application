# Research Engine & Evidence Engine

## Components
- **Research Orchestrator**: Differentiates between casual conversational questions and deep research inquiries requiring hypotheses and evidence synthesis.
- **Evidence Engine**: Formats claims, findings, and external sources with credibility metadata (e.g. higher weighting for academic/peer-reviewed domains) and tracks contradictions.
- **Research Graph**: Maps relationships between questions, findings, claims, and sources across local sessions.
